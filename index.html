The person behind the session

My journey began in community nutrition.

My interest in technology grew from the work I was doing and the questions it raised.

The beginning

People came before programming

I studied Community Nutrition at the University for Development Studies. Working at Shama Health Center and Samartex Hospital brought me closer to the everyday realities of healthcare and community needs.

The questions

Information needed to become useful

As District Nutrition Officer in Agona East, and Head of the Nutrition Department, programme monitoring and reporting strengthened my interest in technology. Could we organize information better? Could we build tools that help people use it?

Learning something new

I became a beginner again

I began developing skills in Python, data science, software development, AI and cloud computing. Digital marketing opened another door: understanding audiences and using performance data to guide improvements.

Where I am now

Working across disciplines

Today, I work as a Digital Marketing Specialist with Amplify Marketing Agency LLC, a USA-based company in Virginia. My work includes SEO, content development, local search visibility and performance analysis.

I am also pursuing an MSc in Computer Science at the University of Ghana. Projects including HealthSignal, MalaGuide and BudgetIQ AI give me opportunities to apply my skills to public health, community support and budgeting.

What you will take away

A foundation you can explain.

  1. Explain the relationship between AI, machine learning and generative AI.
  2. Distinguish supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning.
  3. Describe how models learn from training data and predict on new data.
  4. Interpret a simple demonstration and recognize prediction errors.
  5. Identify applications in healthcare, business and everyday life.
  6. Explain how data quality, bias, privacy and oversight affect responsible use.

Do I need programming experience?

No. This is an introductory session with an exercise you can complete without writing code.

No. The workshop uses fictional records. Please keep private and confidential information out of the activities.
